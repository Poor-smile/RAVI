Google Drive integration

Privacy policy

This policy explains what Google data Raavi can access, why it is used, where it is stored, and how you can remove it.

Effective date August 25, 2026 Applies to Raavi for desktop

The short version

Your Drive stays under your control.

Access
Raavi uses limited, per-file Drive access for backups you enable. It does not ask for access to every file in your Drive.
Storage
Backups travel directly from the desktop app to your Google Drive. OAuth credentials are encrypted on your device.
Deletion
Disconnecting stops Raavi from using the account. To remove existing backups, delete the Raavi folder in Drive and empty Trash.

Scope

This policy covers the optional Google Drive backup feature in the Raavi desktop app. You can use Raavi without connecting a Google account. Local documents and backups that never enter Google Drive are outside the Google Drive part of this policy.

Raavi is developed by Hossien Pouresmaeil. In this policy, "Raavi," "we," and "us" refer to the Raavi application and its developer.

Data Raavi accesses

When you choose Connect with Google, Raavi requests the following OAuth scopes:

  • openid and email, used to identify the connected Google account and show its email address in Raavi.
  • drive.file, used to create and manage Raavi backup files and folders, plus files you explicitly open or share with Raavi.

Raavi also reads your Google Drive display name and storage quota, including total usage and Trash usage. This lets the app show which account is connected and pause media uploads when available space is too low.

Raavi does not receive your Google password and does not request unrestricted access to every file in your Google Drive.

How the data is used

Google account data is used only to connect your account, display connection and storage status, upload the backup categories you select, and keep those backups up to date.

When backup is enabled, Raavi creates this folder structure:

Raavi/
├── Repository/ Markdown documents
├── Assets/     Selected images, audio, and attachments
└── Metadata/   Backup metadata and version information

Depending on your settings, a backup can contain:

  • Markdown document text and structure.
  • Annotations, titles, tags, and document references.
  • Optimized images and their file details.
  • Audio and other attachments, only when you enable them.
  • Backup time, file name, selected backup categories, and retained version information.

Raavi uses a content hash to avoid uploading duplicate assets. It does not use Google user data for advertising or to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Where data is stored

Backup files are sent over HTTPS from the Raavi desktop app directly to Google Drive and are stored in your Google account. Raavi's developer does not operate an intermediary server that receives or stores a separate copy of these backups.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted before Raavi stores them in the app data folder on your device. If secure operating system storage is unavailable, Raavi does not save the connection.

Raavi also keeps local backup state on your device. This can include the connected account email, a recent quota snapshot, sync status, and references used to match local documents to Drive backups.

Retention

Files in Google Drive remain there until you delete them. Disconnecting Raavi or revoking its access does not delete backups already stored in your Drive.

When version history is enabled, Raavi keeps recent text history for up to 30 days or 50 versions. The current version and protected revisions are kept. When version history is disabled, Raavi removes extra non-current, non-protected revisions during backup.

Google may retain deleted files in Trash until you empty it or Google removes them under its own retention rules.

How to disconnect and delete your data

  1. Stop Raavi from using the connected account. In Raavi, open تنظیمات, choose فایل‌ها و کتابخانه, then select قطع اتصال. This removes the encrypted OAuth credentials stored by Raavi on that device. It does not delete existing Drive backups.
  2. Delete the backed-up files. Open Google Drive, delete the top-level Raavi folder, then empty Google Drive Trash if you want the files removed permanently.
  3. Revoke future account access. Open your Google Account's third-party connections, select Raavi, and remove access. Revoking access stops future Google API access, but it does not delete files already saved in Drive.

Sharing and Google API Limited Use

Raavi does not sell Google user data. It does not share Google user data with advertisers or other third parties. Google receives and processes data as needed to provide Google Drive, subject to the Google Privacy Policy in a new tab .

We may disclose information when required by law, or when necessary to investigate abuse, fraud, or a security issue. No other transfer or disclosure is permitted unless we first obtain your explicit consent.

The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy in a new tab , including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

Raavi uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow with PKCE and a short-lived local callback. API requests are sent over HTTPS. OAuth tokens are encrypted on your device using operating system protected storage. Access is limited to the scopes described above.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and Google account protected, and disconnect Raavi if you no longer use Drive backup.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Raavi's Google Drive integration or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
